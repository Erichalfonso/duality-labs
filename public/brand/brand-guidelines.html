Brand Guidelines

Logo

Primary Mark

The Duality Labs logo consists of the Greek letter Psi (Ψ) rendered in Georgia serif typeface. This symbol represents the duality of mind and technology, human creativity and artificial intelligence working together.

Ψ DUALITY LABS
Ψ DUALITY LABS

Clear Space

Maintain clear space around the logo equal to the height of the Psi symbol's vertical stroke. This ensures the logo remains legible and impactful.

Ψ

Logo Misuse

To maintain brand consistency, avoid the following:

  • Don't rotate or skew the logo
  • Don't change the logo colors outside brand palette
  • Don't add effects like shadows or gradients to the symbol
  • Don't stretch or distort the proportions
  • Don't use low contrast color combinations
  • Don't place the logo on busy backgrounds
